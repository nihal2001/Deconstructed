Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

This policy explains what the Deconstructed app collects, why, who handles it, and how to delete it. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

What we collect

DataWhy
Your Apple account identifier, and your email address if Apple shares it. Apple lets you hide your real addressTo sign you in and keep your account separate from everyone else's
Your name, username, and profile settings such as who can see your reviewsTo show your profile and let people find you
Restaurant visits, scores, notes, dishes, photos, saved places and ranking systemsThis is the content of the app. It's stored so that you, and the people you choose, can see it
Who you follow, who follows you and who you blockTo show your feed and enforce your choices
Reports you send or that are made about you, with a copy of the reported contentTo moderate the community
Your location, only if you allow it, and the text of your restaurant searchesTo find restaurants near you. These go to Google to answer the search. We don't store them with your account

Location is optional. If you say no, you can choose an area by hand.

Who sees your content

You choose who can see each review: everyone, your followers, or only you. Public reviews count anonymously towards a restaurant's community scores. Moderators can open anything that has been reported. Your name and username can be seen by other signed-in people.

How long we keep data

  • Your account and content stay until you delete them or your account.
  • Restaurant names and details from Google aren't stored. We store Google's place ID, and a restaurant's coordinates for at most 30 days.
  • Reports stay until they're closed and then for 12 months, including any copy of reported content and the reported person's ID. This also applies after an account or its content has been deleted.
  • If an account is banned, we keep a record of the ban so that it can't simply sign in again.
  • Copies in our hosting provider's backups are overwritten on its normal schedule.

Deleting your data

Open Profile → Delete account in the app. You'll confirm with Apple, and then your profile, visits, ratings, photos, saved places, ranking systems and follows are permanently removed. We also revoke the app's access to your Apple account. To remove a single review or photo, delete it in the app.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, move or delete your data, or to object to how it's used. Most of this can be done in the app. For anything else, use the support page.

Children

Deconstructed isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
